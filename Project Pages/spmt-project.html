Spatial Membrane

Fall 2025

In this project, I manipulated a singular, rectangular surface to create a variety of spaces and interesting pathways for pedestrian use. The focus was on morphing the surface while still maintaining a smooth form in order to recognize the original plane. To make the complex twists, bends, and holes, I learned many new operations in Rhino. By researching how different objects interacted with my surface to instigate the creation of space, I was able to develop areas on the same surface with unique experiences.
