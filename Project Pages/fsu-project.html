FSU Photoshoot

Summer 2024; In collaboration with the FSU Graphics Team

This is a collection of photos taken of football recruits during an official visit at Florida State University. As a team, we set up multiple stations with different backgrounds, lighting, and props. For photos in my station, I was in charge of telling the recruits poses to try, giving them different props, and taking multiple photos from many different angles. Some of these images were then used by the FSU graphics social media team for posting.
